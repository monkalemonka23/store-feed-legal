How the apps are built

The same three rules apply to every one of them.

  • No theme code

    Everything installs through Shopify app blocks in Theme Editor, so nothing is left behind in your theme when you uninstall.

  • No customer data

    Apps request the narrowest set of scopes they need and store no personal data about your shoppers. Detail in the privacy policy.

  • Answered support

    One address, one person behind it: a reply within 24 hours on business days.
