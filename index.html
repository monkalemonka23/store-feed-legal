How the apps are built

The same three rules apply to every one of them.

  • No theme code

    Everything installs through Shopify app blocks in Theme Editor, so nothing is left behind in your theme when you uninstall.

  • No customer data

    Apps request the narrowest set of scopes they need and store no personal data about your shoppers. Detail in the privacy policy.

  • Answered support

    One address, one person behind it: a reply within 24 hours on business days.

Not everything we make is software.

The GrowBoost library on Adobe Stock is mostly AI-made photography, with illustrations and video alongside it. The point is the same as with the apps: make the one picture somebody actually needs and cannot find, rather than a thousand that look like everything else. Everything is licensed through Adobe, so it drops straight into a campaign, a deck, a storefront or a 404 page - this site's own 404 runs on one of them.
